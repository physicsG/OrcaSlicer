Add unused U1 toolheads & ACE spools to the filament list

What's new vs today. The picker groups the machine filaments into U1 Toolheads and ACE — Unit A. T4 is shown once as ● ACE A4 (fed by that slot) — no more duplicate row. Empty heads (T3) offer None. Idle ACE spools (A1) appear under the ACE group. Because the printer gives no head→slot id, the ACE-fed head is matched to its slot by colour+type.

Open question for you: should mapping target the toolhead (what's physically at each head) or the ACE slot directly? In head mode the toolhead is the natural target; in multi mode the ACE slots are (T = ace*4 + slot). The grouping above lets both be expressed.
