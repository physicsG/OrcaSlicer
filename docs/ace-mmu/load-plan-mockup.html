Mockup notes. This is the assignment dialog for U1 + multiACE slicing. Auto runs the swap-minimising optimiser; click any placed spool to pin it to its position (amber badge) — pins are hard constraints and the plan re-optimises live around them. Amber (PETG) starts pinned because A1 is drying it: a drying spool must stay in its ACE. Manual hands you the layout: drag spools between positions (a displaced spool swaps into the old spot) and the dialog prices your layout against the auto optimum; click-to-move works as a fallback. Dragging in Auto pins the spool where it lands. Swap counts come from the same algorithm as the slicer (AceMmuPlan, ported to JS for this demo, exact search).
